Sleeve it, don't leave it

Measure a card, get the right sleeve.

SleeveIt measures any board game card in real millimetres and tells you exactly which sleeves to buy — and how many.

Card sizes differ by just a few millimetres. Buy the wrong sleeve and it's too tight or too loose. SleeveIt takes out the guesswork — measure once, buy exactly what fits.

Features

Everything you need to sleeve a collection

Measure any card with your camera

ARKit projects the card's corners onto the table for a true-millimetre size, then recommends the matching sleeve. A one-time calibration with any bank card tunes it to your exact device. No AR? A reference-photo mode has you covered.

A catalog of 8,800+ games

Search almost any board game and see every deck it contains — card counts and exact sizes, expansions included. Card size data licensed from SleeveYourGames.com.

One consolidated shopping list

My Sleeves rolls everything into a single list — "buy 975 Mini European, 727 Card Game, 298 Standard American." Tell it how many you already own and it shows only what's left to buy.

Track what's sleeved

Mark each game sleeved, in progress, or not started, note the brand you use per size, and watch the progress bar fill.

Reference chart

Every sleeve standard, drawn to scale, with the games that use each one.

Widget & Siri

A home-screen widget shows sleeves to buy at a glance, and Siri answers "How many sleeves do I need?"

How it works

From a loose card to a shopping list in three steps

Measure or search

Scan a card with your camera for a real-millimetre size, or look the game up in the catalog.

Get the exact sleeve

SleeveIt matches it to the right standard — expansions and all — and tells you the count for each deck.

Build your list

My Sleeves combines every game into one shopping list you can track, share as text or PDF, and check off.

Designed to feel right at home on iPhone

About the app

Small, fast, and fully offline

SleeveIt is a native SwiftUI app built with ARKit, Vision, and WidgetKit. The whole catalog ships in the app, so it works on a plane or at the table — and updates itself automatically when you're online. No account, nothing to set up.
